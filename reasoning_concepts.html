Letter Shifting & Positional Value: Standard numbering assigns $A=1, B=2, \dots, Z=26$[cite: 38, 40, 45]. Reverse position is obtained via $(27 - \text{Rank})$[cite: 28, 30].

Fictitious / Message Coding: In multi-word coded sentences, isolate target words by comparing pairs of statements to eliminate common words and their shared codes[cite: 25, 27, 29, 31, 36].

EJOTY Multiples of 5 Rule

Direction Sense & Distance

Cardinal & Intercardinal Directions: North (N), South (S), East (E), West (W)[cite: 25, 28, 30]. Right turn $= 90^\circ$ clockwise; Left turn $= 90^\circ$ counter-clockwise[cite: 24, 25, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 35, 36, 40, 42].

Shadow Rules at Sunrise/Sunset: At sunrise, sun is in the East; shadows always fall to the West[cite: 46]. If a person's shadow is to their right at sunrise, they must be facing South[cite: 46].

Shortest Distance (Pythagoras Theorem)
$$d = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$$
Net Angle of Rotation
$$\theta_{\text{net}} = \sum \theta_{\text{anti-clockwise}} - \sum \theta_{\text{clockwise}}$$
Direction Shifting Hack (PYQ #1924 & #2180): If South-East becomes North ($135^\circ$ anti-clockwise turn)[cite: 26, 42]: apply the exact same $135^\circ$ anti-clockwise shift to the target direction. West shifted by $135^\circ$ anti-clockwise becomes South-East[cite: 42].

Order & Ranking

Position Overlap Principle: When a single person's rank is known from both extremities, their position is counted twice[cite: 30]. Subtract 1 to get total strength[cite: 30].

Position Interchange Model: When two persons swap places, the change in rank of person 1 equals the change in rank of person 2[cite: 42].

Total Strength Formula
$$\text{Total} = (\text{Rank}_{\text{Left}} + \text{Rank}_{\text{Right}}) - 1$$
Position Interchange Total
$$\text{Total} = \text{New Position of } X + \text{Old Position of } Y - 1$$
Instant Total via Symmetrical Rank (PYQ #1987): If someone is $n^{\text{th}}$ from both sides, Total $= 2n - 1$[cite: 30].
If Suresh is $16^{\text{th}}$ from both ends: $2(16) - 1 = 31$[cite: 30].

Syllogisms & Venn Diagrams

Universal & Particular Statements:
1. "All A are B" $\implies$ Some B are A[cite: 25, 37].
2. "No A is B" $\implies$ No B is A.
3. "Some A are B" $\implies$ Some B are A[cite: 25, 37].

$$n(A \cup B) = n(A) + n(B) - n(A \cap B)$$
Immediate Conversion Rule: If "All X are Y" and "All Y are Z", then "All X are Z" and "Some Z are X" must follow unconditionally[cite: 42].

4-Legged & 2-Legged Animals Equation
Let $H = \text{Heads}, L = \text{Legs}$[cite: 32].
$$\text{4-Legged Animals} = \frac{L - 2H}{2} = \frac{L}{2} - H$$[cite: 32] $$\text{2-Legged Animals} = H - \text{(4-Legged)}$$[cite: 32]

Clocks & Calendars

Clock Speeds:
- Minute hand speeds: $6^\circ/\text{minute}$ or $0.1^\circ/\text{second}$[cite: 39].
- Second hand speed: $360^\circ/60\text{ s} = 6^\circ/\text{second}$[cite: 39].
- Relative speed of hands: $5.5^\circ/\text{minute}$.

Angle Between Hands Formula
$$\theta = \left|30H - \frac{11}{2}M\right|$$
Hands Together ($0^\circ$) Time Formula
$$M = \frac{60}{11} \times H \quad \text{minutes past } H$$
Identical Calendar Months: Two months in any ordinary year have identical calendars if the total odd days between them is 0[cite: 34].
April & July: April (30 days $\to$ 2), May (31 $\to$ 3), June (30 $\to$ 2). Sum $= 2 + 3 + 2 = 7 \equiv 0$ odd days[cite: 34]. April 1st falls on the same weekday as July 1st[cite: 34].
