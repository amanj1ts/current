CENTRAL SCHEMES & NATIONAL DEVELOPMENTS
  • GOBARdhan Central Sector Scheme: Petroleum Ministry launched scheme with an outlay of ₹23,731 crore over a 10-year period (FY27 to FY36)[cite: 4]. Introduces benchmark price of ₹2,110/MMBtu; capital subsidy up to ₹2 Cr/TPD; credit guarantee up to 85% (₹20 Cr for MSMEs, ₹25 Cr for women-led units); phased CBG obligations (3% in FY27, 4% in FY28, 5% in FY29+)[cite: 4].
  • Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority (ITLA): Union Cabinet approved ITLA (Special Purpose Vehicle) to prepare a 10+ year National Transport Master Plan, conduct technical appraisals of projects ≥₹500 crore, and manage the National Transport Data Repository (NTDR)[cite: 24, 25].
  • PM-DHARA Scheme: Cabinet cleared ₹1.86 lakh crore scheme targeting completion by FY 2032-33 to evacuate 135 GW renewable power via InSTS and deploy 50 GWh Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)[cite: 33].
  • MSP for Rabi Crops (RMS 2027-28): CCEA approved increases across 6 crops[cite: 31]. Highest absolute hike for Safflower (+₹675/qtl to ₹7,215), followed by Rapeseed/Mustard (+₹413/qtl to ₹6,613)[cite: 31]. Wheat raised to ₹2,610; Lentil (Masur) ₹7,390; Gram ₹5,958; Barley ₹2,286[cite: 31].
  • Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY): MoSJE distributed 662 assistive devices in Delhi-NCR[cite: 8]. Provides free assisted-living devices for BPL senior citizens (income ≤₹15,000/mo; 80+ yrs get doorstep delivery), funded via Senior Citizens' Welfare Fund, implemented by ALIMCO[cite: 8, 9].
  • National Household Travel Survey (NHTS): 1st all-India survey by NSO (MoSPI)[cite: 9]. 66.4% of students (6+) travel >1 km daily (walking 37%, bus 29.5%, bicycle 15.8%)[cite: 9]; 57.6% workers commute >1 km (two-wheelers lead at 42.6%)[cite: 9]; urban mass transit at 5% (Maharashtra highest at 20.7%)[cite: 9].
  • JOSH Teams Initiative: Health Ministry, with Sports and Defence Ministries, launched Joint Squad for Health in 55 districts across 11 states for youth-led TB elimination[cite: 13].
  • Press Council of India (PCI): I&B Ministry notified 13 journalists[cite: 25]. PCI is a statutory, quasi-judicial body under Press Council Act 1978; comprises Chairman and 28 members[cite: 25].
  • Sengol in Education: NCERT added Sengol into Class 9 Social Science textbooks; derived from Tamil word 'Semmai' (righteous governance)[cite: 50].
