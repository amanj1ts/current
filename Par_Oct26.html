UNESCO TENTATIVE SITES & HERITAGE FOCUS
  • India's UNESCO Tentative Count: Rose to 78 sites with 5 new additions (MP accounts for 19 sites): 1. Bagh Cave Paintings: 9 sandstone Buddhist caves (4th–6th century CE) along Baghni River (MP); Cave 4 (Rang Mahal) features post-Ajanta Jataka murals.
    2. Chambal Valley Temples: Early medieval Nagara-style temples (8th–11th century CE) across Bateshwar (Gurjara-Pratihara), Padavali, and Kakanmath (Kachchhapaghata ruler Kirtiraj) in Morena (MP).
    3. Udaigiri Caves: 20 Gupta-period rock-cut caves near Vidisha (MP); Cave 5 features monumental Varaha relief saving Bhudevi; Sanskrit inscriptions of Chandragupta II & Kumaragupta I; early modak-holding Ganesha in Cave 6.
    4. Ginnorgarh Hill-Fort: Seat of Gadha/Garha Gond kingdom (King Sangramsah; Rani Durgavati) in Ratapani WLS featuring ruined 7-storey Rani Mahal and Gond courtly paintings.
    5. Sites of Satyagraha (Transnational Proposal): Tracing Mahatma Gandhi across 9 sites (2 in South Africa: Pietermaritzburg & Phoenix Settlement; 7 in India: Champaran Smarak, Sabarmati, Mani Bhavan, Harijan Sevak Sangh, Sevagram, Aga Khan Palace, Gandhi Smriti).
  • The Sengol in NCERT: NCERT Class 9 textbook added the Sengol (derived from Tamil 'Semmai' meaning justice, truth, and righteous governance). Traces Chola coronation tradition; crafted by Vummidi Bangaru Chetty for Nehru in 1947; installed in Parliament 2023.
  • Anant Chaturdashi: Observed on Chaturdashi Tithi of Shukla Paksha in Bhadrapada month; concluding phase of Ganeshotsav dedicated to Lord Vishnu in Anant form.
