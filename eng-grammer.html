Direct Punjabi equivalents and high-frequency PSSSB/NITTTR pairs

Person vs Thing (Angry / Annoyed)

• Angry with a person (He was angry with me)[cite: 47].
• Angry at a thing/conduct (He was angry at my behavior)[cite: 47].
• Annoyed with a person vs Annoyed at an action[cite: 47].

Accountable / Responsible / Obliged

• Accountable to a person / authority[cite: 47].
• Accountable for actions/duties[cite: 47].
• Obliged to a person; Obliged for a kindness[cite: 49].

Charged with vs Accused of

• Accused of a crime (Never accused with)[cite: 47].
• Charged with a crime (He was charged with murder)[cite: 48].
• Acquitted of all charges (Exonerated from)[cite: 47, 49].
