Ten Gurus Quick Exam Points

• Guru Nanak: Kartarpur (Ravi), Langar, 3 Pillars, 4 Udasis, Babarvani (1520).

• Guru Angad: Gurmukhi script standardization, Mall Akhara, Khadur Sahib.

• Guru Amar Das: 22 Manjis, Goindwal Baoli (84 steps), Anand Sahib, abolished Sati.

• Guru Ram Das: Founded Ramdaspura (Amritsar), 4 Lavan hymns, Masand system.

• Guru Arjan: Harimandir Sahib (Mian Mir, 1588), compiled Adi Granth (1604), 1st Martyr (1606).

• Guru Hargobind: Miri-Piri, Akal Takht (1606-09), Lohgarh Fort, 4 battles.

• Guru Har Rai: Herbal dispensary, peaceful cavalry of 2,200 horsemen.

• Guru Harkrishan: 'Bal Guru' (accession age 5), served smallpox in Delhi (Bangla Sahib).

• Guru Tegh Bahadur: 'Hind Di Chadar', founded Anandpur (1665), martyred at Chandni Chowk (1675).

• Guru Gobind Singh: Khalsa Panth (1699), 5 Ks, Panj Pyare, Chamkaur, Zafarnama, sealed Guru Granth Sahib (1708).
