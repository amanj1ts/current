1. Making of the Constitution & Constituent Assembly
🔥 Top Repeat Historical Genesis
Assembly Formation & Officers

Formed Under: Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946

Seats: Total 389 (296 British India + 93 Princely States); reduced to 299 post-partition.

1st Meeting: Dec 9, 1946 — Interim President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha.

Permanent President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (elected Dec 11, 1946).

Vice-Presidents: H.C. Mukherjee & V.T. Krishnamachari.

Constitutional Advisor: Sir B.N. Rau (prepared the initial draft).

Objectives Resolution: Moved by Jawaharlal Nehru (Dec 13, 1946); adopted Jan 22, 1947.

Calligrapher: Prem Behari Narain Raizada (Artwork by Nandalal Bose).

Drafting Committee & Timeline

Drafting Committee Appointed: August 29, 1947 | Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

7 Members: (1) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, (2) N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, (3) Alladi Krishnaswamy Iyer, (4) Dr. K.M. Munshi, (5) Syed Mohammad Saadulla, (6) N. Madhava Rau (replaced B.L. Mitter), (7) T.T. Krishnamachari (replaced D.P. Khaitan).

Duration: 2 Years, 11 Months, 18 Days (11 Sessions) | Adoption: Nov 26, 1949 | Enforcement: Jan 26, 1950.

Symbols: Flag: July 22, 1947 | Anthem & Song: Jan 24, 1950.

3. Part I: The Union & Its Territory (Articles 1–4)
🦁 Punjab & PEPSU 📰 Art 3 Renaming
Article 1: India, that is Bharat, shall be a "Union of States" (Indestructible union of destructible states).
Article 2: Admission or establishment of new states not part of India.
Article 3: Formation of new States, alteration of boundaries or names of existing States.
📰 Current Affairs Linkage — Renaming of Kerala to "Keralam": Proviso to Art 3 requires prior recommendation of President. Referred to State Assembly for views within time; state views are purely consultative and not binding on Parliament.
Article 4: Laws made under Art 2 & 3 require only Simple Majority and are NOT amendments under Art 368.
Punjab & State Reorganisation Facts:
  • PEPSU (Patiala and East Punjab States Union): Formed 15 July 1948 (Part B state; Rajpramukh: Yadavindra Singh; Premier: Gian Singh Rarewala). Merged into Punjab in 1956 (7th CAA).
  • Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966: Shah Commission created Haryana (17th state, 1 Nov 1966), UT Chandigarh (joint capital), and transferred hill areas to Himachal Pradesh.
  • Accessions: Junagadh (Plebiscite), Hyderabad (Operation Polo, Sept 1948), J&K (IOA signed 26 Oct 1947 by Hari Singh).
4. Part II: Citizenship (Articles 5–11) Single Citizenship
Article 5–8: Citizenship provisions at commencement (26 Jan 1950).
Article 9: Single Citizenship: Persons voluntarily acquiring foreign citizenship automatically lose Indian citizenship.
Article 11: Parliament's Power: Parliament has plenary power to regulate citizenship by law (Citizenship Act 1955).
5. Part III: Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35)
🔥 Top Weightage 📰 SC Expansions
Article 12 & 13: Art 12: Definition of 'State'. Art 13: Laws inconsistent with FRs void; includes Doctrine of Severability and Doctrine of Eclipse.
Articles 14, 15, 16: Right to Equality. Art 14: Equality before law (UK) & Equal protection (USA). Art 15: Non-discrimination. Art 16: Public employment.
📰 Current Affairs — Quota & Creamy Layer: SC held unreserved seats are purely merit-based, not a general-category quota. Parental income alone cannot determine OBC creamy layer exclusion.
Article 17 & 18: Art 17: Abolition of Untouchability (Absolute right). Art 18: Abolition of titles.
Article 19: 6 Freedoms: Speech 19(1)(a), Assembly 19(1)(b), Associations 19(1)(c), Movement 19(1)(d), Residence 19(1)(e), Profession 19(1)(g). Art 19(1)(f) deleted by 44th CAA 1978.
Article 20: Protection against conviction: 20(1) No ex-post-facto law, 20(2) No Double Jeopardy, 20(3) No Self-Incrimination.
Article 21: Protection of Life & Liberty: Procedure established by law must be just, fair, reasonable (Maneka Gandhi 1978).
📰 Article 21 Expansions in News: Access to Menstrual Hygiene in schools declared facet of dignity; Bodily/reproductive autonomy of minors recognized; Highway commuter safety & pedestrian footpaths held actionable rights; Passive euthanasia reaffirmed (Harish Rana case).
Article 21A: Right to Education (6–14 years): Added by 86th CAA 2002.
Articles 22–24: Art 22: Detention safeguards (magistrate within 24 hrs). Art 23: Human trafficking & begar prohibited. Art 24: Child labour banned below 14 yrs.
Articles 25–30: Freedom of Religion (25–28). Minority Rights (29–30) for 6 notified national minorities.
Article 32: Right to Constitutional Remedies: "Heart and Soul of the Constitution" (Dr. B.R. Ambedkar). High Courts issue under Art 226.
The 5 Prerogative Writs (Articles 32 & 226)
WritLiteral MeaningKey Scope & Target

Direct Article Numbers 📰 State UCC Enactments
Article 38 & 39: Art 38: Social welfare & reduce income inequalities (44th CAA). Art 39(d): Equal pay for equal work.
Article 39A: Equal justice and free legal aid (42nd CAA 1976).
📰 Current Affairs: SC Judge Justice Surya Kant named Executive Chairman of NALSA; launch of AI legal bot Nyaya Setu under DISHA.
Article 40: Organisation of Village Panchayats (Gandhian Principle).
Article 44: Uniform Civil Code (UCC) for all citizens.

Key Duties: 51A(a) National Flag/Anthem, 51A(h) Scientific temper, 51A(k) Education for child aged 6–14 yrs.
7. Part V: The Union Executive & Parliament High Yield
Article 52–58: President of India. Electoral College (Art 54): Elected MPs + Elected MLAs (including Delhi, Puducherry, J&K). Age: 35 years.
Article 61: Impeachment of President: Ground: "Violation of Constitution". Requires 14 days' notice (1/4th members) and 2/3rd total membership of each House.
Article 63–71: VP is ex-officio RS Chairman. Art 71: Election disputes decided solely by Supreme Court.
Article 72: Pardoning Powers of President: Pardon, Commutation, Remission, Respite, Reprieve (covers death sentences & courts-martial).
Article 75: PM appointed by President. 75(1A): Ministers capped at 15% of Lok Sabha (91st CAA). 75(3): Collective Responsibility to Lok Sabha.
Article 76: Attorney-General for India: Appointed by President; qualified to be SC Judge. Audience in all courts; can speak in Parliament without right to vote.
Article 87: President's Special Joint Address: At start of first session after general election & first session each year (Budget Session).
Articles 94(c) & 96: Speaker removal by Effective Majority (majority of then members, vacancies excluded). Speaker cannot preside during removal proceedings.
Article 102(2) & 10th Schedule: Disqualification on defection. Exemption applies if not less than 2/3rd of legislative party merges.
Article 108: Joint Sitting: Summoned by President; presided over by Lok Sabha Speaker. (Ordinary & Financial bills only; NOT for Money or Amendment bills).
Article 110 & 114: Art 110: Money Bill certified by Speaker; RS gets 14 days. Art 114: Appropriation Bill required to withdraw from Consolidated Fund.
Article 123: President's Ordinance Power: During recess; max life: 6 months + 6 weeks.
8. Judiciary: Supreme Court & High Courts 📰 Appointments & Benches
Article 124: Supreme Court (Inaugurated 28 Jan 1950). Retirement age: 65 years.
📰 Current Affairs: Justice B.R. Gavai sworn in as the 52nd Chief Justice of India (First Buddhist CJI); speeches compiled in "The Voice of Justice".
Articles 129, 131, 141, 142: 129: Court of Record. 131: Original jurisdiction (federal disputes). 141: SC law binding on all courts. 142: Power to do "complete justice".
Article 143: Advisory Jurisdiction: President can refer questions of law/fact to SC.
📰 Current Affairs: Presidential reference under Art 143 on judicial directives fixing timelines for Governors/President to clear State Bills.
Articles 148–151: CAG of India: Appointed by President; tenure 6 yrs or 65 yrs. Vetted by Public Accounts Committee (PAC).
High Courts (Articles 214–231): Judges retire at 62 years. Appointed under Art 217; transferred under Art 222. Common HC under Art 231 (Punjab & Haryana HC).
📰 Current Affairs Updates: Permanent bench of J&K and Ladakh HC established in Ladakh; Justice Lisa Gill appointed 1st woman CJ of Andhra Pradesh HC; Justice Revati Mohite Dere appointed 1st woman CJ of Meghalaya HC; Sikkim became India's first paperless judiciary.
9. Part VI: State Executive & Legislature
🦁 Punjab Vidhan Sabha 📰 Art 200 Assent
Article 153–156: Governor appointed by President; holds office during pleasure of President. Age: 35 yrs.
Article 161: Governor pardoning power; cannot pardon death penalty or court-martial sentences.
Article 165: Advocate-General for the State: Highest law officer of state; appointed by Governor.
Article 169: Abolition/creation of Legislative Councils by Parliament (Punjab Council abolished 1970).
Article 200: Assent to Bills by Governor:
📰 Landmark SC Ruling & Assent: In State of Punjab v. Governor, SC held Governor cannot sit indefinitely on bills and must return them as soon as possible; Punjab Governor gave assent to Jaagat Jot Sri Guru Granth Sahib Satkar (Amendment) Bill.
Article 213: Governor's Ordinance power during Assembly recess (valid for 6 weeks from reassembly).
10. Parts IX & IX-A: Local Self-Government 🔥 Guaranteed PSSSB MCQs
Pillars: Lord Ripon (1882 - Father of Local Govt) | Balwant Rai Mehta (1957 - 3-tier model) | 1st adopted in Nagaur, Rajasthan (2 Oct 1959) | 73rd CAA came into force: 24 April 1993 (Panchayati Raj Day).
Article 243A: Gram Sabha: Body of registered village electors.
Article 243B & 243C: 3-tier structure (Intermediate tier exempt if population < 20 Lakhs).
Article 243D: Reservation: Minimum 1/3rd (33%) for women (Punjab has 50%). SC/ST proportionate to population.
Article 243E & 243F: 5-year duration; min contesting age is 21 years.
Articles 243F & 243V: Disqualifications:
📰 Current Affairs: Telangana and Rajasthan repealed their statutory two-child disqualification for contesting local body polls.
Article 243G & 243W: 11th Schedule: 29 Subjects (Panchayats); 12th Schedule: 18 Subjects (Municipalities).
Article 243-I & 243K: SFC set up by Governor every 5 yrs; SEC conducts elections (removed like HC judge).
Article 243M & PESA Act 1996: Extends Part IX to 5th Schedule areas based on Bhuria Committee.
📰 Current Affairs: Jharkhand formally notified its PESA Rules 25 years after statehood, empowering Gram Sabhas.
11. Parts XI & XII: Centre-State, Water & Finance 🦁 Federalism & SYL
Article 246A: Simultaneous legislative powers for GST (101st CAA 2016).
Article 248 & 249: Residuary powers with Parliament. Art 249: Parliament legislates on State subject on 2/3rd RS resolution.
Article 262: Inter-State River Water Disputes: Parliament can exclude jurisdiction of Supreme Court (Crucial for SYL Canal Dispute).
Article 263: Inter-State Council: Set up 1990 (Sarkaria Commission); chaired by Prime Minister.
Article 280: Finance Commission: Appointed every 5 yrs by President (Chairman + 4 members).
📰 16th Finance Commission Final Report (2026–2031): Submitted by Chairman Dr. Arvind Panagariya.
Article 300A: Right to Property: Legal / Constitutional Right (44th CAA 1978).
Article 312: All India Services created only via Rajya Sabha 2/3rd resolution.
Article 315–323: PSCs: UPSC members retire at 65; SPSC at 62. SPSC appointed by Governor, removed ONLY by President.

Article 324: ECI conducts Parliament, Assembly, President, VP elections.
📰 CEC Removal Safeguard (Art 324(5)): Chief Election Commissioner removed in like manner and grounds as an SC Judge.

Article 338, 338A, 338B: NCSC (Art 338); NCST (Art 338A - 89th CAA); NCBC (Art 338B - 102nd CAA, headed by Sadhvi Niranjan Jyoti).

📰 Landmark Verdicts: 7-Judge bench upheld state power to sub-classify SCs/STs (e.g., Valmikis & Mazhabi Sikhs in Punjab); SC affirmed SC status under 1950 Order restricted to Hinduism, Sikhism, or Buddhism.
Official Languages (Part XVII): Art 343: Hindi in Devanagari script. Art 348: Language in SC/HC is English.

13. Emergency Provisions, Key Amendments & Landmark Cases Direct Questions
Article 352: National Emergency: War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion. Approved within 1 month by Special Majority.
📰 Observance: 25 June observed as Samvidhaan Hatya Diwas marking the 1975 Emergency.
Article 356: President's Rule: Approved within 2 months by Simple Majority. Max limit: 3 yrs. Imposed first in Punjab (1951).
📰 Current Affairs: Imposition in Manipur with Assembly in suspended animation.
Article 358 & 359: Articles 20 and 21 can NEVER be suspended under any emergency (44th CAA 1978). Art 360 (Financial Emergency) never invoked.
