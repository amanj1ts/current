State Schemes, Budget & Governance

  • Mukh Mantri Sehat Yojana (MMSY): Punjab Govt rolled out a universal cashless health scheme providing cashless treatment up to ₹10 Lakh per family/year.

  • Punjab Budget 2026-27: Presented by Finance Minister Harpal Singh Cheema. Fiscal deficit pegged at 4.08% of GSDP; effective revenue deficit at 2.06%.
  • Punjab Sikhya Kranti 2.0: Next-phase school education reform rolled out in direct partnership with the World Bank.
  • Mission Samarth 4.0: State foundational learning flagship program upgraded with a state-wide real-time attendance tracking platform.
  • First-Ever Drugs & Caste Census: Multi-department head-count initiated in Punjab to map drug abuse extent and socio-demographic profiles.

Infrastructure, Renaming & Legal Acts

  • Sri Guru Ravidas Ji Airport: Adampur Airport (Jalandhar) officially renamed as 'Sri Guru Ravidas Ji' Airport by PM Modi; civil terminal at Halwara Airport (Ludhiana) inaugurated.
  • Sacrilege Law Amendments: Assent given to Jaagat Jot Sri Guru Granth Sahib Satkar (Amendment) Bill 2026; mandates enhanced punishments up to life imprisonment and fines of ₹5 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh for 'beadbi'.
  • Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur World Class University: Punjab Vidhan Sabha unanimously passed Bill 2026 to set up this university at Sri Anandpur Sahib (9th Guru's sacred seat).
  • North India's First Glass Bridge: To be constructed over Sutlej Lake at Nangal in Ropar (Roopnagar) district.
  • Sri Guru Tegh Bahadur Wildlife Sanctuary: Punjab State Board for Wildlife renamed Jhajjar-Bachauli Wildlife Sanctuary (Rupnagar) along the Sutlej river; ₹5.36 crore eco-plan approved.
  • Shahpur Kandi Dam: Located on the Ravi River in Pathankot district (downstream of Ranjit Sagar Dam); operationalized to halt surplus water outflow into Pakistan.

Security & Operations

  • Operation Parhar: Massive crackdown launched by Punjab Police to dismantle state gangster networks (12,000 cops, 2,000 search teams).
  • Operation Shield: Inter-agency security & border mock drill conducted across vulnerable districts.
  • New Vigilance Bureau Chief: Senior IPS officer Sharad Satya Chauhan appointed chief, replacing P.K. Sinha.

Heritage, Sikh History & Culture

  • Guru Angad Dev Ji: Jyoti-Jyot Diwas observed; 2nd Sikh Guru born as Bhai Lehna at Matte-di-Sarai (Sarai Naga, Muktsar).
  • Operation Blue Star: 42nd anniversary commemorated on June 6, 2026 (conducted June 1984 at Sri Harmandir Sahib, Amritsar).

Chandigarh & Tri-City Flashpoints

  • Chandigarh Fully Literate: Achieved 99.93% literacy rate; became 2nd UT and 6th overall State/UT in India to achieve full literacy.
  • Chandigarh State Butterfly: State Board for Wildlife declared the Striped Tiger Butterfly as Chandigarh's official State Butterfly.
